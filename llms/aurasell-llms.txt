# Aurasell

> Aurasell is an AI-native go-to-market (GTM) platform that unifies the entire revenue lifecycle — prospect, sell, manage, and operate — into a single intelligent system. Aurasell either replaces legacy CRMs (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho) for startups and SMBs, or layers an AI-native GTM Operating System on top of an existing CRM for mid-market and enterprise teams.

## What Aurasell does

Modern sales teams juggle 15+ disconnected tools — territory management, account intelligence, sequencing, conversational intelligence, CPQ, forecasting, dashboards — creating data silos, context switching, and admin overhead. Industry data Aurasell cites: 91% of organizations miss sales quota, 70% of rep time is lost to admin work, and only 30% of B2B reps hit quota.

Aurasell consolidates these capabilities into a single AI-native platform built on a unified data model, with the stated outcomes of:

- 80% reduction in manual tasks across the sales cycle
- 35% increase in sales velocity
- 50% reduction in ramp time
- 2x increase in selling capacity

The platform organizes around four motions: **Prospect, Sell, Manage, Operate.**

## Two deployment paths

Aurasell offers two ways to adopt the platform depending on company stage:

1. **AI-Native GTM Platform** (startups & SMB): Aurasell becomes the GTM platform, built on an Intelligent CRM. Replaces Salesforce, HubSpot, or Zoho.
2. **AI-Native GTM OS** (mid-market & enterprise): Aurasell layers on top of an existing Salesforce or HubSpot CRM as an AI-powered GTM operating system, no rip-and-replace required.

## Positioning summary

Aurasell's category claim is the **AI-Native GTM Platform** — a category distinct from legacy CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot), sales engagement (Outreach, Salesloft), conversational intelligence (Gong, Chorus), CPQ (Salesforce CPQ, DealHub), and forecasting (Clari, BoostUp). Rather than being one of those tools, Aurasell consolidates the work they do into a single AI-native system built on a unified data model, with native AI agents handling research, outreach, call prep, note-taking, quoting, coaching, and forecasting work that reps would otherwise do manually across multiple tools.
